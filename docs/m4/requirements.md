## Policy decisions recorded here (pending owner confirmation)

- **Settlement**: two providers. Payment requires byte-identical revealed `bool[]` vectors. Any difference fails the job and opens a creator refund. _Anthony to confirm._
- **Third provider**: not implemented. Proposed decision: no third-provider fallback this semester. A disagreement ends in refund, not escalation. _Anthony and Chris to confirm._
- **Network**: local Anvil (chain ID 31337) only. No public testnet deployment exists. _Anthony to confirm._
- **Wallet identity**: users sign in with email/password. Wallets are only used to sign transactions and to prove provider ownership. No private key is ever entered on the website. _Chris to confirm the challenge design._
